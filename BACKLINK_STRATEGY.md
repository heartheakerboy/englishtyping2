# High-Impact White-Hat Backlink & Authority Strategy
## EnglishTypingTest.org (Educational & Typing Niche)

---

### Executive Overview
Search engines rank typing test platforms primarily on user engagement (low bounce, high dwell time) combined with natural, high-trust contextual backlinks from educational institutions, career portals, exam preparation sites, and tech/developer communities.

This document outlines a sustainable, 100% white-hat backlink strategy to establish `https://www.englishtypingtest.org` as an authoritative global reference for typing assessments.

---

### 1. Educational & University Outreach (.edu / .ac / Schools)
**Target**: High school computer labs, university career services, coding academies, community colleges.

- **Resource Page Placement**:
  - Millions of schools maintain "Student Resources" or "Digital Literacy" reference pages linking to typing tools.
  - Outreach Pitch: Introduce `englishtypingtest.org` as a completely free, fast-loading, privacy-focused typing tool with no login barrier, zero distracting popups, and dedicated 60-second to 15-minute lessons.
- **Career Preparation Centers**:
  - University placement cells guide students preparing for administrative, legal, customer support, and medical transcription jobs requiring verified WPM.
  - Value Proposition: Introduce the instant **Verified Typing Certificate** feature (`/certificate/$id`) that students can link directly on their resumes and LinkedIn profiles.

---

### 2. Government & Competitive Exam Preparation Hubs
**Target**: SSC CGL/CHSL candidates, GCC-TBC Maharashtra institutes, Stenographer prep forums, Sarkari exam blogs.

- **Dedicated Exam Simulators**:
  - `englishtypingtest.org/ssc-cgl-typing-test` (15-min / 2000 keystrokes with official Full vs. Half mistake deductions).
  - `englishtypingtest.org/gcc-tbc-typing-test` (7-min 30 & 40 WPM passages with accurate error penalty rules).
- **Outreach Execution**:
  - Partner with coaching institutes, Telegram study groups (100k+ members), and educational bloggers covering SSC/State exam syllabus.
  - Offer coaching centers customized practice tests (`/test/$slug`) that teachers can create for their students for free.

---

### 3. Digital PR & Data-Driven Journalism
**Target**: Tech, productivity, and workplace journalists (Lifehacker, MakeUseOf, Hacker Noon, Business Insider, Fast Company).

- **"Global Typing Speed & Productivity Benchmark" Report**:
  - Aggregate anonymized metrics (e.g., average WPM across 500,000+ test sessions by country, keyboard layout, time of day, and mobile vs. desktop).
  - Key finding angles that earn organic news citations:
    - *"How Remote Work Affected Global Typing Speeds"*
    - *"QWERTY vs. Dvorak: Is Alternative Typing Actually Faster in 2026?"*
    - *"Average Customer Support WPM by Region"*
- **Infographics & Press Kit**:
  - Create high-resolution WPM distribution charts and statistical graphics journalists can embed with attribution links.

---

### 4. Interactive Typing Badges & Developer Widgets
**Target**: GitHub profile READMEs, personal developer portfolios, tech blogs.

- **Embeddable WPM Badge**:
  - Allow users to display dynamic shields.io-compatible SVG badges:
    `[![Typing Speed](https://img.shields.io/badge/Typing_Speed-85_WPM-brightgreen)](https://www.englishtypingtest.org)`
  - Creates compounding viral exposure across thousands of developer repositories and tech portfolios.
- **IFrame Embeds for Classroom & Coding Portals**:
  - Provide lightweight iframe code for educators to embed 60-second drills directly inside school LMS portals (Canvas, Google Classroom, Moodle).

---

### 5. Enthusiast Community & Broken Link Building
**Target**: r/MechanicalKeyboards, r/typing, Keybr/Monkeytype forums, Geekhack, Deskthority.

- **Niche Hardware & Layout Enthusiasts**:
  - Support specialized keyboard features (e.g., Colemak, Dvorak, split keyboard ergonomics tutorials on `/blog`).
  - Sponsor community typing speed sprints and tournaments with leaderboard recognition.
- **Broken Link Replacement**:
  - Scan popular university and library resource guides for dead links to discontinued typing sites (such as old Flash-based typing games or defunct typing test tools).
  - Send polite notices to webmasters informing them of the broken links and suggesting `https://www.englishtypingtest.org` as a modern, mobile-friendly HTML5 alternative.
